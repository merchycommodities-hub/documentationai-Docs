---
title: How to Create a New Commodity Listing
description: Quick guide to adding a new product for sale.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Login to Dashboard">
    ````
    Go to https://merchycommodities.com/login and access your account.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Listings">
    ````
    Click "My Listings" in sidebar then click "Add New Listing".
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Product Details">
    ````
    Fill in commodity name, quantity, description, and price.
    Upload clear product photos.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    ````
    Click "Publish". Your product is now live for buyers.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
