---
title: Can't Log In to My Account
description: Fix account login problems.
---

## Symptoms

- Invalid credentials error
- Forgotten password

## Quick Fixes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset password">
    ````
    Click "Forgot Password" at login page and follow email link.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Email">
    ````
    Look in spam/junk if reset email does not arrive.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    ````
    Email support@merchycommodities.com for manual reset.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
