## Shapes and Sizes

Buyers often compare size, density, and shape before they ask for pricing. Keep this section direct and easy to scan. If you support custom production, explain the minimum order quantity and the approval process.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Standard Shapes" icon="package">
    
    Standard shapes are best when you want predictable production and repeatable quality. Use them for buyers who need fast turnaround and familiar export packaging.

    Common examples include:

    - Hexagonal sticks
    - Cubes
    - Pillow briquettes
    - Round or pressed forms

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Custom Shapes" icon="settings">
    
    Custom shapes help you support private label orders and regional market preferences. You can describe available mold sizes, approved tolerances, and sample approval steps.

    Include details such as:

    - Length and diameter
    - Corner profile
    - Surface finish
    - Minimum order quantity

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Size Guide" icon="bar-chart">
    
    Use a size guide to help buyers compare options before requesting a quote. For example, you can list the nominal size, target weight, and packaging count per carton.

    | Format | Nominal Size | Notes |
    |---|---|---|
    | Small | 25 mm | Good for compact retail packs |
    | Medium | 26 mm to 28 mm | Common export default |
    | Large | 30 mm and above | Often used for premium orders |

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Spec Sheet Reference

Use a spec sheet to make your catalog more practical. A buyer can compare your product against internal procurement requirements, and your sales team can respond faster with consistent information.

| Field | Example Entry | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Moisture content | Below 8% | Helps buyers judge storage and performance |
| Ash content | Low ash finish | Supports cleaner use and better presentation |
| Fixed carbon | Export grade | Shows combustion quality |
| Burning time | Stable and consistent | Important for user experience |
| Packaging | Retail or bulk cartons | Affects logistics and branding |

<Callout kind="tip">

If you offer multiple grades, keep the catalog format identical for each grade. That makes it easier for buyers to compare options and request the correct quote.

</Callout>
